## Page
Treatment: panels 1 to 3 look like frames from a hand-painted 1980s cel-animated film: painted backgrounds, noon light under a cobalt sky, thin clean ink, two-tone cel shadows, and no halftone. Panel 4 switches to flat fauvist color in which the blue house is the only blue (a lime sea under a magenta and tangerine sky). Panel 5 is night in two blues. The photograph in panel 3 is a real sepia photograph. Five panels.

## Panel 1
Art: Top strip, full width. A high camera on Alder Street behind the mint-green Tercel, looking down at the T-junction: the car turns right onto Shore Road, its right turn signal lit. On the right-hand corner, as on THE CORNER sheet: the STOP sign post with the SHORE RD and ALDER ST blades, the yellow hydrant, the white picket fence, and the bougainvillea. Beyond: the seawall, the beach, and the bay. The chapter title sits on a cream ribbon banner at the top left. A small inset breaks the panel border: Nami at the wheel, yawning.
Title: Chapter 2 · Up the Hill
Caption: Right on Shore Road, then right again, up the hill.
Caption: On Mondays she drives it mostly asleep.

## Panel 2
Art: Middle left. A hilltop campus parking lot; a sign reads LOT C; the Tercel, with its rust and dented bumper, sits alone in the far corner, shaded by a huge eucalyptus.
Caption: Lot C. Her corner.
Caption: The walk up takes forty minutes. She did it once.

## Panel 3
Art: Middle right. A classroom. Professor Ruiz (a short, energetic woman in her sixties with a silver bob, big round glasses, and a cardigan with pens in the pocket) holds up a large print with both hands. The print is a real sepia photograph: a dirt track along the coast with a horse standing in the middle of it, facing the camera, ears pinned back, glaring.
Ruiz: Shore Road, 1911.
Caption: A dirt track. The horse looks annoyed.

## Panel 4
Art: Bottom, the largest panel, flat fauvist color. Noon on a low stone wall above the lot. Nami, in profile with her eye open, leans forward and looks down at the town, holding a steaming plain white paper cup of noodles. The sea is lime; among the rooftops below, one small house with blue walls and a dark gray roof is the only blue.
Caption: From the wall she can find her blue house.
Caption: She can't tell if the door is locked. She looks anyway.

## Panel 5
