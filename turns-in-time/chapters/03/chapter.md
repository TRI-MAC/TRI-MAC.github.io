## Page
Treatment: a minimalist day in thick sea fog. The page uses only three flat colors: warm fog-gray paper, ink black, and pearl lavender. Lines are few and confident, with wide empty fields of fog. Lavender belongs only to the strange car and its faint sheen. Everything else is paper tone and black line, including the house, the Tercel, the sign, Nami's cardigan, the caption boxes, and the balloons. Nami's hair is solid ink black. There is no halftone and no misregistration. Panel 2 breaks the quiet with black ink speed lines and a jagged burst but adds no color. Panel 4 is a tight close-up with almost no background. Four panels.
